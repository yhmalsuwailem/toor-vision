TOOR VISION / DETECTOR — RESEARCH DEMO

counter.mp4 and service.mp4
Café: A Data Set for Complex Activity Recognition, ECCV 2024.
Dongkeun Kim, Youngkil Song, Minsu Cho, Suha Kwak.
https://dk-kim.github.io/CAFE/
https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.02878
Official public archive, cameras 6 (clips 70–79) and 19 (clips 220–229).
Real camera footage with research participants performing activities; not generated imagery or customer testimonials.
Sequences were reconstructed at the provided annotated sampling intervals, then encoded at 25 fps. Each sequence contains ten source clips. The elapsed time between source clips is not validated. Rules reset timers, role memory and vacancy state at every source-clip boundary. Manual role corrections are scoped to a source clip.
The dataset archive did not include an explicit redistribution license in its inspected index. The website's CC BY-SA notice must not be represented as a verified license for the dataset archive. Unrestricted commercial redistribution is not established.

INFERENCE
RF-DETR Small (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/roboflow/rf-detr
ByteTrack through Supervision (MIT): https://github.com/roboflow/supervision
Actual inference at 5 fps. Three videos, 808 sampled frames. Model and video SHA-256 hashes, hardware, bounding boxes and confidences are in each JSON file.
The browser replays computed results. It does not run a neural network locally or claim a live camera connection. Roles are configurable region rules, not model predictions. Track IDs are not unique-person counts. Proximity does not prove service, missing detections do not prove absence, and the model does not establish employee intent.

BENCHMARK
20 supplied human-labelled keyframes across cameras 6 and 19, 213 labelled person boxes; no fine tuning. Confidence >= 0.45, IoU >= 0.5, one-to-one greedy matching in descending confidence.
RF-DETR Small: TP 136, FP 68, FN 77; precision 66.7%, recall 63.8%.
RF-DETR Medium: TP 134, FP 71, FN 79; precision 65.4%, recall 62.9%.
This selected sample is not full-dataset mAP and does not measure tracking, service, attendance, staff-role correctness or fairness. Some mismatches reflect occluded/amodal annotation extents. Full figures are in benchmark.json.
