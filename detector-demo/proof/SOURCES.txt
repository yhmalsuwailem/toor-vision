REPRODUCIBILITY
Each JSON contains source URL, output video SHA256, model SHA256, generated time,
GPU, library versions, sampling rate and per-frame detection boxes and tracker IDs.
775 sampled frames across four clips; 5 inference samples per second.
Generated by experiments/sales-demo/generate.py, not hand-animated boxes.
The hosted/offline demo replays precomputed model output. Rules run in the browser.

LIMITS
These are public sample clips, not a labeled benchmark for customer-service events.
No service accuracy, precision/recall or identity accuracy is claimed.
Occlusion can lose or split tracks. Role labels come from configured starting zones
or user correction, never biometric recognition. Proximity is a screen-space proxy,
not proof of assistance. No phone/device handover or deliberate-delay recognition.
Demonstration thresholds are shortened for short clips, not operating recommendations.

No inference is made about actual employee performance in the source videos.
